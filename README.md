# Hi, I'm Anthony

MBA student who builds data tools for things I care about: trading and fitness. Everything here is a passion project, built to answer a real question I had.

## Projects

**[trading-research](https://github.com/acmworkflow-stack/trading-research)** – A backtesting and validation framework for Nasdaq and gold futures. Walk-forward tests, overfitting checks (PBO, deflated Sharpe), cost stress tests and a lookahead check. The main result: 13 strategy families tested on 3 years of data showed no edge after costs. Includes a Dash dashboard with a gamma exposure map and an implied volatility surface.

**[morning-briefing](https://github.com/acmworkflow-stack/morning-briefing)** – A one-page morning market brief: futures quotes, headlines with optional FinBERT sentiment, an economic calendar, SEC filings and FRED data.

**[FITNESSV2](https://github.com/acmworkflow-stack/FITNESSV2)** – A mobile-first lift tracker I built for my personal-training clients. It logs weight and reps per set, tracks personal records, shows last week's numbers for each lift, and exports everything to Excel.

**[HoH-Milli](https://github.com/acmworkflow-stack/HoH-Milli)** – A web app I installed on my phone to replace a paid coaching app (Trainerize) for my family. It tracked lifts and reps and had an early food and calorie log. I stopped building it once the food database outgrew what I could maintain by hand.
